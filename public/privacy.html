Privacy policy

Mandarin Leitner · last updated 11 October 2026

In short: the app has no server and no account with us. Your cards and progress are stored on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on sync with your own Google Drive or use AI import with an AI service you choose. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your data is never sold or shared.

Who we are

Mandarin Leitner is a free, open-source flashcard app made by an individual developer (GitHub: ErfanDejband). It runs entirely in your web browser and is served as static files from GitHub Pages at https://erfandejband.github.io/chinese_flash_card/.

Data stored on your device

Your cards (characters, pinyin, meaning, notes, pictures), your review progress and history, and your settings are kept in your browser's storage on your device. They do not leave the device unless you use one of the optional features below or export a backup file yourself. Deleting the app's site data in your browser deletes them.

Sync with Google Drive (optional)

If you connect Google Drive in Settings, the app signs you in with Google and asks for two permissions:

  • See, create and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata). The app stores a copy of your cards, review progress and history, import records (file names and page numbers) and settings in a hidden folder of your own Google Drive that only this app can open. The app cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive. AI keys and your speech-voice choice are not synced.
  • See your email address (email), only to show which Google account is connected and to pre-select it when you sign in again.

This data goes directly between your browser and Google; it is never sent to the developer or anyone else, and the developer has no access to it. The sign-in token is kept in your browser and expires after about an hour.

Mandarin Leitner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to provide sync to you: it is not used for advertising, not sold or transferred to others, not used to train AI models, and not read by people.

To stop and remove it:

  • In the app, Settings → Sync between devices → Disconnect stops syncing on that device.
  • To delete the copy in your Drive: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Mandarin Leitner → Delete hidden app data.
  • To remove the app's access to your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

AI import (optional)

If you use AI import, the pages, photos or screenshots you select (and any text in them) are sent directly from your browser to the AI service you chose in Settings, such as Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, OpenRouter, Groq, OpenAI, or a server on your own computer. That service processes them under its own privacy policy; some free tiers may use submitted content to improve their products, so don't import private documents. API keys, or the key received when you connect OpenRouter, are stored only in your browser and are sent only to their own service. They are never included in backups or sync.

Other things to know

  • Pronunciation uses your device's or browser's text-to-speech; some browsers may use an online voice service to speak the text.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the app's files, may keep standard web server logs such as IP addresses (see GitHub's privacy statement).
  • The app is not directed at children under 13.
  • If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
