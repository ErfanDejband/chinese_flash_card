學 Mandarin Leitner

A free flashcard app for learning Mandarin vocabulary (Traditional characters and pinyin) with the Leitner box system.

Open the app
  • Leitner boxes: cards you know move up and come back less often; cards you forget start again.
  • Three practice directions: picture → Chinese, Chinese → pinyin and meaning, and listening (the word is spoken by your device), each with its own progress.
  • Progress: daily streak, activity calendar, weekly numbers and your most-forgotten words.
  • Import from course material: optionally let an AI model of your choice read PDFs, photos or screenshots and suggest cards, which you check before they are added.
  • Works offline and installs on Android and desktop as an app.

Your data stays yours

Everything is stored in your browser on your device. There is no account with us, no server, no tracking and no ads. If you want your phone and laptop to stay in step, you can turn on sync with Google Drive: the app then keeps a copy of your cards and progress in a hidden folder of your own Google Drive that only this app can open. It cannot see any other file in your Drive. That is why the app asks you to sign in with Google.
